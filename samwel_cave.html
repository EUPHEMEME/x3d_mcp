Samwel Cave — Wintu sawal, "sacred place," the Cave of the Lost Maiden. McCloud River, Shasta County. Surveyed by Furlong, Merriam & Sinclair (1903–06); cross-section after Feranec et al. (2007). Sister cave to Potter Creek. Now on the shore of Shasta Lake, on flooded Winnemem Wintu homeland. Chamber layout, the Magic Pools, and the ~90 ft hole connecting the upper and lower levels are documented; room sizes, exact pit geometry, speleothems and lighting are interpretive.
