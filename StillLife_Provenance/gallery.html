Still life, with provenance

Three scenes authored in X3D 4.0, each grounded in a named public-domain painting and each carrying its own provenance in-scene. They are interpretive — inspired by the paintings, not reproductions of them — and the scenes say so in their own metadata rather than in a caption.

What silently failed

24 documented failure modes. The ones that matter are those where the XML validated and the render was still wrong — no schema catches those. Two examples from this build: X_ITE ignores the shadows field on lights entirely (verified by rendering the same scene with it on and off and getting byte-identical output), and x3d.py's serializer omits containerField='value' on MetadataSet members, so the provenance payload itself shipped misparented until it was patched and asserted.
